Process

Four steps, no surprises

You see the full landed cost before anything moves. No arrival-day surcharges, no "pending" line items.

  1. 01

    Scope & quote

    We review commodity, Incoterms, volume and timeline, then return an all-in landed cost: freight, duty, handling and clearance in one figure.

  2. 02

    Documents & compliance

    Classification is confirmed, licences and certificates are collected, and the entry is pre-validated against destination rules before departure.

  3. 03

    Book & track

    Space is secured with a contracted carrier and every milestone is pushed to you: gate-in, sailing, transhipment, arrival, release.

  4. 04

    Clear & deliver

    Entry is filed ahead of arrival so the container moves on release day, straight to your door or into bonded storage.

About

Built by people who have cleared the cargo themselves

Vertex was founded in 2014 by a small team of customs brokers and ocean operators who were tired of watching shipments stall in the gaps between vendors. The fix was not more software. It was putting freight, brokerage and warehousing under one roof, with one person accountable per account.

Today we move cargo for manufacturers, distributors and retailers across more than 120 countries, from single-pallet LCL consolidations to multi-container industrial programmes. We are deliberately mid-sized: large enough to hold real carrier contracts, small enough that your coordinator knows your cargo without looking it up.

FAQ

Questions we get before the first booking

What regions do you ship to?

More than 120 countries through a vetted network of carriers, agents and bonded facilities. Lane density is heaviest across Asia-Pacific, North America, Europe and East Africa.

Do you handle customs clearance as well as freight?

Yes. We are a licensed customs broker. Classification, valuation, duty calculation, entry filing and post-entry corrections are all handled in-house, so freight and clearance never sit with two different companies pointing at each other.

What is the minimum shipment size you accept?

There is no minimum. We consolidate LCL cargo from a single pallet and scale up to multi-container programmes and chartered air uplift.

How quickly can I get a quote?

Standard lane quotes come back within one business day. Special handling, hazardous goods or project cargo usually takes two to three business days because we price those against a live carrier response.

Can you work with our existing suppliers?

Of course. We will onboard your suppliers, audit their export documentation and coordinate pickups directly with them. If you would rather we source, we can do that too.

How do you charge?

One all-in landed cost per shipment, or a contracted rate card for recurring volume. Duty and government fees are passed through at cost with documentation attached.
